**Name:** Card Chest

## Description

Card Chest is a private collection vault for trading cards — starting with Pokémon and already friendly to Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic, sports, and more from your PriceCharting export.

Open **Today** and see the clearest next steps: sell, grade, buy/watch, refresh prices, or scan a card. Portfolio value, P/L, and comps stay on your Mac.

• One-time purchase — no subscription, no account  
• Sell Hub with fee-adjusted net estimates and listing kits  
• Grade Lab with break-even math  
• Scanner with the full TCGdex English + Japanese codex  
• Arcade pulls from your real vault  
• Optional bring-your-own-key live prices & AI — never required  

Your vault. Your machine. Yours forever.

## Review notes

This app manages a user’s local trading-card collection export. It does not scrape third-party marketplaces. Optional API keys are entered by the user and stored only on device. Network calls are limited to card images (TCGdex) and user-enabled BYOK providers.
